Memory Clinic

Comprehensive cognitive assessment and personalized care for memory concerns

Comprehensive Memory Assessment

A thorough evaluation of cognitive functions, including memory, attention, language, and executive functions, to identify potential concerns and establish a baseline for brain health.

Duration: 2-3 hours

Personalized Cognitive Rehabilitation

Tailored programs designed to improve specific cognitive skills through targeted exercises, strategies, and compensatory techniques, guided by neuroscience principles.

Duration: 12-week program (1-hour sessions, twice weekly)

Brain Health Lifestyle Coaching

One-on-one coaching to develop and implement sustainable lifestyle changes that support optimal brain health, covering nutrition, exercise, sleep, stress management, and social engagement.

Duration: 6-month program (bi-weekly 45-minute sessions)

Advanced Neuroimaging Consultation

Expert interpretation and consultation on advanced neuroimaging results (e.g., fMRI, PET scans) to provide deeper insights into brain structure and function related to cognitive health.

Duration: 1-hour consultation

CMS GUIDE Caregiver Support

Our Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services GUIDE program provides comprehensive dementia care coordination and support for patients and their families.

  • Personalized care planning and coordination
  • 24/7 caregiver support and resources
  • Evidence-based interventions and treatments
  • Comprehensive family education and training
